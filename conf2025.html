April 12, 2024

Re x Design

view site ↗

Re-xDesign invites us to challenge past narratives, critically examine the present, and imagine new futures through design. The three panels - Reclaim, Realize, and Reimagine - will engage in discussions that span across time, questioning the narratives we inherit, the systems we operate within, and the possibilities we can create.
